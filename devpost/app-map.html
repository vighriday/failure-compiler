Failure Compiler

This app turns a messy distribution plan into confirmed facts, tests every single and pair problem, and lets the checker decide whether a change fixes the selected case without new failures among the tested scenarios.

One real action

Load the example, confirm the map, open a broken case, and read the concrete morning shown for that result. The schedule is an explanation; the verdict compares the locked outcome fields, so tied schedules do not change it.

To change this, start here

  • Change the scenario set or locked count: server/checker/scenarios.py and tests/test_example_locked.py.
  • Change what counts as an equivalent verdict: server/evaluation.py and tests/test_evaluation.py.
  • Change the visible timeline wording: web/src/components/CaseTimeline.tsx.
  • Change the source-citation contract: server/validation/models.py, server/ai/prompts.py and tests/test_fact_sources.py.

A practice to reuse

When the solver produced different but equally good schedules, the build separated observable verdict fields from explanatory metadata. The evidence is recorded in devpost/ai-reading-review.md and enforced by tests/test_evaluation.py. On another project, define the equivalence fields before comparing generated outputs.
